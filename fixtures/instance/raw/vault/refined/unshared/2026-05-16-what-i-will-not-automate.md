There is a category of work I have decided to keep doing by hand, and the
category is not defined by difficulty. It is defined by whether doing the work
is how I find out what I think.

Deciding what to read next is in this category. Writing the first paragraph of
anything is in it. Choosing which of my projects gets the next month is very
much in it, and every time I have tried to build a system that ranks them for me
I have ended up arguing with the ranking, which is just the choosing again with
extra steps and a worse mood.

What I will happily automate is everything that stands between me and those
decisions: gathering, joining, remembering, counting. Those are chores and I am
bad at them in a way no amount of discipline has fixed. The line is not manual
versus automatic. It is whether the doing is also the thinking.
