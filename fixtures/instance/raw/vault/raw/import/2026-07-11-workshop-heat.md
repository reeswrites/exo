Ranking my own projects by when I last touched them tells me almost nothing.
Half of them were started in the same fortnight, so last-touched order mostly
records which one I happened to open on a Sunday, not which one I have actually
been living inside.

Volume inside a window is the honest measure. Thirty small commits over two
months is a project I am in; one commit yesterday is a project I remembered.
The second reads as fresher on every dashboard I have built, which is how I
know the dashboards were measuring the wrong thing.

I also want a word for "finished" that is not "dormant". Most of what looks
abandoned in my workshop simply shipped, and a heat model that cannot tell those
apart will keep nagging me to revive things that are done.
