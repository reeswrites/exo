If a machine is going to hand my own thinking back to me, I want it cut and
never reworded. A summary of my notes is a new document with a new author, and I
have no way to audit the places where the summary is confidently wrong about
what I meant.

Verbatim spans have an unglamorous virtue: I can check them. The span either
appears in the note or it does not, the note either exists or it does not, and
any disagreement resolves in seconds instead of turning into an argument about
interpretation. Machines may rearrange my sentences. They may not write new ones
and sign my name.

The cost is that verbatim retrieval is worse at flattery. It cannot smooth over
the fact that I said something muddled in April. I consider that a feature, and
most of the systems I have tried consider it a bug to be fixed.
