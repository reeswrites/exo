Promoted from a conversation. My half only.

A rating scale is a private language and publishing the number without the
grammar is worse than publishing nothing. If my median restaurant score is eight
point one, then an eight is a shrug, and a reader who assumes a ten-point scale
is centred at five will read every shrug I have ever recorded as enthusiasm.

The fix is not to recentre my ratings. I rate the way I rate and rescaling them
after the fact would destroy the only thing they are good for, which is
comparing me to me. The fix is to ship the calibration alongside the numbers and
insist that anything quoting one quotes the other.
