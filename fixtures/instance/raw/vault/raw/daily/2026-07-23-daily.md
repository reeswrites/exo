A good day, and I want to record why rather than just that. I worked on one
thing for four uninterrupted hours, and the reason I could is that I decided the
night before what the one thing would be. No part of the morning was spent
choosing.

I notice I only ever manage this after a week that went badly. The bad week
supplies the motivation and then the motivation runs out around Thursday, which
means my best working days are downstream of my worst ones in a way I would
rather not depend on.

If I can make the choosing happen the night before as a habit rather than as a
reaction, I think most of the rest of it follows. That is the whole experiment
for August.
