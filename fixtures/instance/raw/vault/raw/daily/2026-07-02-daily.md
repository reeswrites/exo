I slept badly and spent the first hour of the morning being annoyed about a
conversation from Tuesday that nobody else has thought about since. I know the
shape of this by now: I am not upset about the conversation, I am upset that I
did not say the clearer version of my point until four hours later.

The thing I actually want to write down is that I keep mistaking the delay for a
failure of intelligence when it is a failure of nerve. I had the clearer version
at the time. I did not say it because saying it would have meant disagreeing
with someone who was enjoying being right, and I decided in the moment that the
disagreement was not worth the temperature.

I am not sure that was wrong, exactly. But I would like to stop filing it under
"I think slowly" when the honest label is "I chose the quiet option", because
those two have completely different remedies and I have been treating the wrong
one for years.
