# The second copy is where the lie starts

I have started refusing to keep a second copy of anything I can rebuild. Not out of
tidiness — out of a distrust of copies that drift. A second copy is a promise to keep
two things equal, and I have never once kept that promise past the third month.

The rule I have landed on is that a copy is allowed if losing it costs a rebuild
rather than a fact. Derived tables, indexes, caches, thumbnails: all fine, all
disposable, none of them the record.
