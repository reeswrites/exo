This is the thing I cook when I have not been to a shop in a week and refuse to admit
it. Everything here keeps for months, and the whole argument is that the anchovies
melt into the oil until nobody can tell they were ever there.

Ingredients:

- 200g spaghetti
- 6 anchovy fillets in oil
- 2 fat cloves of garlic, sliced thin
- 1 dried chilli, crumbled
- 1 tin of good chopped tomatoes
- a handful of stale breadcrumbs
- parsley, if you have it

1. Put the pasta on in water saltier than you think reasonable.
2. Warm the oil gently and melt the anchovies into it with the garlic and chilli. Do
   not let the garlic colour.
3. Add the tomatoes and reduce hard for eight minutes until the sauce goes glossy.
4. Toss the drained pasta through with a ladle of its water, off the heat.
5. Fry the breadcrumbs in a dry pan and scatter over with the parsley.

Notes:

- Tinned tomatoes vary more than anything else in this list. Buy the boring
  expensive ones.
- Do not add cheese. It fights the anchovy and the anchovy loses.
