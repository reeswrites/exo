Braises are the only cooking where doing less is genuinely better, and this one asks
almost nothing after the first twenty minutes. Make it the day before if you can; it
is better reheated and nobody has ever disagreed with me about that.

Ingredients:

- 1.2kg shoulder, cut into large pieces
- 2 onions, sliced
- 1 tbsp ras el hanout
- half a preserved lemon, chopped, skin and all
- 500ml stock
- coriander to finish

1. Brown the meat hard in batches and set aside. Do not crowd the pan.
2. Sweat the onions in the same fat until soft, then add the spice and cook it out
   for a minute.
3. Return the meat, add the preserved lemon and the stock, and bring it barely to a
   simmer.
4. Cover and hold it there for ninety minutes, checking twice.
5. Rest for twenty minutes off the heat before serving over couscous.

Tips:

- Half the ras el hanout and double the preserved lemon if you like it sharper. I do.
