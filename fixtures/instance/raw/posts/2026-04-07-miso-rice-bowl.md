The dressing is the recipe. Make double, keep it in a jar, and the rest of this is
assembly. I have eaten a version of this bowl most weeks for two years and the only
change has been making more pickles.

Ingredients:

- 300g short-grain rice
- 3 tbsp white miso
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 2 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 cucumber, sliced thin
- 2 eggs
- scallions to finish

1. Rinse and cook the rice.
2. Whisk the miso, vinegar, mirin and sesame oil until it emulsifies and looks like
   thin custard.
3. Salt the cucumber, wait ten minutes, then squeeze and dress with a spoonful of the
   vinegar.
4. Boil the eggs for six and a half minutes and peel them under cold water.
5. Build the bowl warm, dress heavily, and finish with scallion.

Substitution ideas:

- Any vinegar works. Any miso works, though red miso wants half the quantity.
